Wayfare AI — Key Flow Wireframes

Low-fidelity wireframes for the 4 screens named in the PRD's "Design — Key Flows" section — intentionally boxy and grayscale, since this is a wireframe fidelity level, not a finished UI.

Scope note: these are the P0 requirements only (destination intent, itinerary generation, budget optimizer) — see the full PRD (T2) for the Requirements table, Goals & Non-Goals, and Launch Plan these screens map to.
