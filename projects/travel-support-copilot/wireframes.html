Travel Support AI Copilot — Key Flow Wireframes

Low-fidelity wireframes for the 4 screens that carry this design's guardrail architecture — intentionally boxy and grayscale, since this is a wireframe fidelity level, not a finished UI.

Scope note: see the full case study (T5) for the conversation-flow transcripts, agent architecture, evaluation approach, and risks these screens map to.
Why these 4 screens specifically: each one exists to close a gap that shows up repeatedly in travel support bots today — most either can't take real action at all (pure FAQ bots) or take action with no visible reasoning, no confirmation step, and no memory once a human takes over. These screens are the direct answer to those four failure modes, not generic chat-UI decoration.

Reading it: Screens 1 and 2 are what the traveller sees; Screens 3 and 4 are what makes the system safe to operate at all — a handoff a human agent can act on immediately, and a log support ops can audit. A copilot that only gets Screens 1–2 right still fails the business the moment something needs to go to a human or get reviewed after the fact.
