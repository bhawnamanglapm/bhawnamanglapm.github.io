Flight Delay Prediction Dashboard

Which routes delay the most, how delay patterns differ by airline, and how delays trend by season and time of day — analyzed from a modeled domestic flight dataset (8,000 flights).

Synthetic, modeled data — not a real airline's published performance. Airlines here are fictional labels (IndiFly, Metro Express, BlueSky Air, Skyline National, Coastal Wings) so no numbers are attributed to a real, identifiable carrier. Routes use real Indian city pairs, since hub congestion (Delhi/Mumbai/ Bengaluru) and seasonal effects (North Indian winter fog, western-coast monsoon) are well-documented public aviation-industry patterns, not a claim about any one company. Full generator + methodology in the repo (see footer link).
