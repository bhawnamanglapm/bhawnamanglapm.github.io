Design principles

Every screen is reviewed against these.

  • Deadline firstEvery dispute view leads with time left and the next action.
  • Plain languageReason codes, statuses and errors are written for merchants, not specialists.
  • One primary actionEach screen and table row has a single clear primary button.
  • No dead endsEvery error and empty state explains what happened and what to do next.
  • Accessible by defaultMeaning never relies on color alone; forms have visible labels; dialogs trap focus. Target: WCAG 2.1 AA.
  • Trustworthy dataShow when data was last synced and label anything provisional.
  • Explainable AIAI suggestions show their sources and reasons, and people decide on high-value actions.
  • Consistent patternsSame header, table, badge and button patterns on every screen.
